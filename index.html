This site supports the YouTube channel Calgary Council and Committee Meetings (@calgarycouncilmeetings), a neutral, unofficial, non-commercial archive of public Calgary City Council and committee meeting recordings.

What the channel does

  • Archives public meeting recordings sourced from the City of Calgary’s public eScribe / ISILive webcasts.
  • Relays live streams of council and committee meetings when they are broadcast publicly.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API only so the channel owner’s own uploader can upload videos, set titles and descriptions, and manage playlists on this channel.

Disclaimer. This channel and website are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Calgary. Meeting recordings remain public records of the City; this project republishes them for convenient public access.
