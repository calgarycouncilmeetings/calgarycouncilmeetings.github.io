This Privacy Policy describes how the Google Cloud OAuth application YYC Council Uploader (“the App”) and this website relate to personal information. The App supports the YouTube channel Calgary Council and Committee Meetings.

What the App does

The App uses YouTube API Services so the channel owner can upload videos, set titles and descriptions, and manage playlists on the channel owner’s own YouTube channel. It does not let other users sign in.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or share anyone else’s personal data. It is used only by the channel owner to operate the channel. OAuth tokens that authorize the App are stored privately on the operator’s computer and are used only to call YouTube API Services for that channel.

This static website does not run accounts, forms that submit data to us, or analytics. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to put in your message.

How YouTube and Google handle data

Use of the App and the channel involves YouTube and Google. Their handling of information is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App’s access to a Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact and corrections

Questions, privacy concerns, takedown requests, or correction requests: calgarycouncilmeetings@gmail.com. Requests are acted on promptly.
