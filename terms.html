These Terms of Use apply to this website and describe the purpose of the YouTube channel Calgary Council and Committee Meetings (@calgarycouncilmeetings) and the Google Cloud OAuth application YYC Council Uploader.

Nature of the project

  • The channel is a neutral, unofficial, non-commercial archive of public Calgary City Council and committee meeting recordings.
  • Recordings are sourced from the City of Calgary’s public eScribe / ISILive webcasts. The channel may also relay live streams of meetings when they are broadcast publicly.
  • This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Calgary.

The App

YYC Council Uploader uses YouTube API Services only so the channel owner can upload videos, set titles and descriptions, and manage playlists on the channel owner’s own YouTube channel. It does not provide sign-in for other users. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
